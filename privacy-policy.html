Maya Sort: Water Color Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Developer: Studio Creative_IO

This policy explains what information the Android game Maya Sort: Water Color Puzzle ("the game") uses, and why. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.

Information stored on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings and whether you bought "Remove ads") is saved on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android also keeps a private copy in your own Google account backup, so your progress comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. The developer cannot see this backup, and your progress is not sent to the developer.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate (country/region-level) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and diagnostic information.

Google uses this to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it (for example, which levels are too hard), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records game events such as starting, finishing or getting stuck on a level, ads watched and purchases, together with device information (model, operating system, language), an app-instance identifier and your approximate country. This information is not used to identify you personally. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics data is not stored unless consent is given. See Firebase privacy information.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase was made, so that it can remove ads and restore the purchase if you reinstall. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

The "Share" button opens WhatsApp (or another app you choose) with a short message about your level. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

The developer does not sell your information. Apart from the Google services described above (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play), no information is shared with third parties.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

Data stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
